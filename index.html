    How the math works
    Sell Price = IC Pay ÷ (1 - Margin). $1,000 ÷ 0.60 = $1,666.67 at 40%
    IC Pay = Sell Price × (1 - Margin). $1,666.67 × 0.60 = $1,000.00
    Margin = (Sell Price - IC Pay) ÷ Sell Price. ($1,666.67 - $1,000) ÷ $1,666.67 = 40%
    Margin is figured on the sell price, not on cost. A 40% margin is a 66.7% markup.
