📐 Active Formulas
From Vendor Cost:
Customer Price = Vendor Cost ÷ (1 - Margin Rate)
Example: $1,000 ÷ (1 - 0.40) = $1,666.67
From Customer Price:
Vendor Cost = Customer Price × (1 - Margin Rate)
Example: $1,666.67 × (1 - 0.40) = $1,000.00
From Margin Rate:
Margin = (Customer Price - Vendor Cost) ÷ Customer Price
Example: ($1,666.67 - $1,000) ÷ $1,666.67 = 40%
